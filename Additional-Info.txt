PNGs are made by batch exporting the "Cursor" page in Inkscape.
These are all the suffixes with their corresponding DPIs:
22: 8.25 DPI
24: 9 DPI
28: 10.5 DPI
32: 12 DPI
40: 15 DPI
48: 18 DPI
56: 21 DPI
64: 24 DPI
72: 27 DPI
80: 30 DPI
88: 33 DPI
96: 36 DPI

"cursorBase" has a drop shadow applied to it. The following are the drop shadows' properties:
Blur radius: 2px
Horizontal and vertical offset: 0.5px
Shadow type: Outer
Blur color: 000000ff
Blur color (light): ffffffff
